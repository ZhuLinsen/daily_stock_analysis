## Overview

DSA reports should feel like a modern institutional analyst dashboard, not a retail newsletter or a broker ad. The contract is optimized for Ben-facing generated artifacts: web dashboards, PDF reports, PNG exports, Telegram-style cards, and compact status cards.

The default voice is English-only, concise, evidence-first, and free of CJK text in generated Ben-facing artifacts unless a caller explicitly asks for another language. Visual output should emphasize decision quality, risk, market regime, confidence, and source-backed reasoning.

## Colors

The palette uses cold institutional neutrals, deep ink text, and a restrained cyan accent. Use the accent to orient attention, not to decorate every surface.

- **Primary (#0B1220):** deep ink for headers, table headings, and high-authority blocks.
- **Surface (#F8FAFC) and surface-raised (#FFFFFF):** clean analyst-workbench backgrounds for PDFs, dashboards, and cards.
- **Accent (#0E7490):** restrained cyan for active states, key callouts, and selected metrics.
- **Positive (#047857), negative (#B91C1C), warning (#B45309):** semantic signal colors for risk, direction, and alerts.
- **Border (#D8E0EA):** quiet grid and card separation. Avoid heavy outlines.

## Typography

Use Inter for prose and UI labels. Use JetBrains Mono only for ticker symbols, ratios, prices, and large metric values. Keep hierarchy sharp: one dominant headline, compact section labels, and readable body text.

PDFs and PNGs must prefer dense but breathable spacing. Do not shrink text below caption size to cram content; summarize instead.

## Layout

Reports use a dashboard grid with a clear top summary, then thesis, risk, evidence, and action sections. Cards should fit one idea per panel. Tables must keep numeric columns aligned and label columns short.

Use `spacing.md` as the normal card interior rhythm, `spacing.lg` for section separation, and `spacing.xl` only for hero-level whitespace. PNG exports should keep the same spacing scale so screenshots match PDF and web artifacts.

## Elevation & Depth

Prefer flat surfaces with subtle separation. Use raised white cards on the page surface, thin borders, and minimal shadow. Avoid glassmorphism, neon glow, playful gradients, and chartjunk. If a generator cannot render subtle depth reliably, fall back to flat cards with border lines.

## Shapes

Use modest rounding: `rounded.md` for cards and tiles, `rounded.sm` for labels, chips, and headers, and `rounded.lg` only for outer report containers. Do not use pill-heavy retail-fintech styling for analyst output.

## Components

- **Dashboard shell:** the outer report canvas; sets the neutral background and base text color.
- **Report card:** the default panel for thesis, risk, catalyst, and evidence blocks.
- **Section header:** high-authority header strip for major report sections.
- **Metric tile:** compact KPI block for price, score, risk, confidence, volume, and flow metrics.
- **Insight callout:** one high-priority takeaway or alert. Use sparingly.
- **Signal labels:** positive, negative, and warning labels for recommendations and risk flags.
- **Chart panel:** neutral chart/table surface with muted supporting labels.
- **Metadata caption:** quiet timestamp, model, source, and caveat text.
- **Divider line:** quiet separator for dense PDF or card layouts.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:

- Produce English-only Ben-facing artifacts by default.
- Lead with the decision, confidence, and invalidation conditions.
- Use semantic colors consistently for signal direction and risk.
- Keep citations and evidence close to the claims they support.
- Optimize cards and PNGs for quick scanning on mobile without losing institutional tone.

Don't:

- Emit CJK text in Ben-facing generated artifacts unless explicitly requested.
- Use meme-stock colors, casino green/red walls, emoji-heavy labels, or marketing copy.
- Invent chart decorations that do not encode data.
- Hide caveats in footnotes when they affect the decision.
- Mix unrelated visual systems between web, PDF, PNG, and notification cards.
